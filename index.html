📋 About the real test (from the USCIS document)
  • The officer asks you up to 20 of these 128 questions out loud. You must answer at least 12 correctly.
  • 65/20 special consideration: if you’re 65+ and have been a permanent resident for 20+ years, study only the 20 ★ questions. You’ll be asked 10 and need 6 correct, and you may take the test in your language.
  • Some answers change with elections and appointments. Answer with the official serving at the time of your interview. Check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.
